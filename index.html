What is the Self?

The "Self" refers to a person's essential being—the distinct combination of psychological, physical, and social qualities that make a person who they are.

01

Self-Awareness

Recognizing your strengths, limitations, emotions, and values forms the baseline for personal growth.

02

Holistic Growth

Understanding that you are made of multiple interconnected aspects—from your physical health to online behavior.

03

Social Connection

Learning how your inner self interacts with communities, relationships, and digital spaces around you.

The 6 Dimensions of the Self

Educational concepts alongside practical daily life scenarios.

Body & Health

Physical Self

Concept: Biological processes, body image, nutrition, and wellness.

Daily Life Scenario: Choosing to get 8 hours of sleep before an exam rather than pulling an all-nighter.
Feelings & Moods

Emotional Self

Concept: Understanding, expressing, and regulating emotions.

Daily Life Scenario: Taking a deep breath and stepping away for a minute when feeling overwhelmed by schoolwork.
Mind & Thoughts

Intellectual Self

Concept: Critical thinking, problem-solving, and curiosity.

Daily Life Scenario: Reading articles about new technology or solving logic puzzles to challenge your brain.
Relationships

Social Self

Concept: Interactions with family, friends, culture, and society.

Daily Life Scenario: Collaborating effectively in group projects and respecting cultural differences.
Purpose & Values

Spiritual Self

Concept: Personal ethics, inner peace, and life purpose.

Daily Life Scenario: Practicing daily mindfulness meditation and acting with integrity in difficult choices.
Online Identity

Digital Self

Concept: Persona, etiquette, and footprint across social media.

Daily Life Scenario: Setting screen-time boundaries on apps and maintaining privacy online.
